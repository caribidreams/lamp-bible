# Lamp: KJV Bible Study — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Lamp is made by Maurice John, Kingstown, St. Vincent and the Grenadines.
Questions about this policy: [maurice@caribidreams.com](mailto:maurice@caribidreams.com).

## The short version

- Lamp has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- The Bible text and study material are on your device. Reading, searching and studying
  work without a connection and send nothing anywhere.
- What you make (highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading plans, saved answers and
  conversations) stays on your device and, if you sign in, in your own iCloud. We cannot
  read it.
- One optional feature, Cloud answers, sends your question off the device. It is off until
  you turn it on. What it sends, and to whom, is set out below.

## What stays on your device

Everything you read, search for and study. Answers to questions are written on your device
by Apple's on-device model unless you turn Cloud answers on. Your notes and highlights can
be found through your device's own search; that index is kept on the device.

## Signing in and iCloud

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. Lamp asks Apple for your name only,
never your email address. Your name is kept on your device and is shown to the people you
read a shared plan with.

When you are signed in, what you make is kept in your private iCloud so that it appears on
your other devices. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

## Reading plans you share

If you share a reading plan, the plan, and each member's name and the days they have
finished, are kept in the iCloud of the person who shared it. Anyone who has the plan's
link can join and see them. Notifications about a shared plan are delivered by Apple; Lamp
has no notification server.

## Cloud answers

Cloud answers is off by default and needs you to be signed in. When it is on, answers are
written by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, instead of on your device.

**What is sent**

- Your question, and the King James passages Lamp found for it.
- For a follow-up, the last two questions and answers of that conversation.
- For a reading plan asked for in your own words, those words.

Your notes, highlights, bookmarks and name are never sent. The request carries no name,
email address or account identifier. Please do not put anything in a question that you
would not want sent.

**Where it goes**

The request goes to Lamp's relay, a small service we run on Cloudflare, which passes it to
Anthropic and passes the answer back. The relay keeps nothing: no questions, no answers and
no record of who asked.

The first time you use Cloud answers, Lamp sends the relay a sign-in token from Apple, once,
to confirm that the request comes from Lamp. The relay checks it, does not keep it, and
gives your device a pass that lasts about six months.

Like any service on the internet, the relay sees your device's internet address while it
handles a request. It uses it only to limit how many requests one address can make, and
does not record it.

**What Anthropic does with it**

Anthropic receives the request from the relay, not from your device. Anthropic states that
it deletes what is sent through its API within 30 days and does not use it to train its
models. It may keep a request for longer where the law requires it, or where its automated
safety systems flag the request as breaking its usage policy (up to two years, and the
safety scores for up to seven). See
[Anthropic's privacy information](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data).

Anthropic and Cloudflare may process requests in the United States and in other countries
where they operate.

You can turn Cloud answers off at any time in Library, Account and Sync. Answers are then
written on your device again.

## When you write to us

If you report a problem or email us, we receive your email address and what you chose to
send: your comment and what you were looking at (the question and answer, or the verse and
name). We use it only to reply and to fix the problem, and delete it when it is no longer
needed.

## Information from Apple

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives us
crash reports and usage figures that do not identify you. Lamp itself collects none.

Tapping a place's map link opens Apple Maps, which is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Children

Lamp does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

- **Use Lamp without signing in.** Everything then stays on the device.
- **Turn Cloud answers off**, or never turn it on.
- **Sign out** in Library, Account and Sync. To stop using Sign in with Apple for Lamp
  altogether, go to Settings, your name, Sign in with Apple.
- **Delete what you have made.** Deleting Lamp removes it from the device. To remove it from
  iCloud as well, go to Settings, your name, iCloud, and manage storage for Lamp.
- **Ask us** what we hold about you. Unless you have written to us, the answer is nothing.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change to
what leaves your device will also be described in the app's release notes.
