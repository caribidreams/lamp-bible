# Support

Lamp is a King James Bible study app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## Contact

Email [maurice@caribidreams.com](mailto:maurice@caribidreams.com). Please say which device
you are using and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Reporting a wrong answer, name or profile

In Lamp, choose Report a Problem on the answer, name or profile. The report includes what
you were looking at and nothing else from your notes or highlights.

## Common questions

**My notes are not on my other device.** Sign in with Apple in My Hub, under Account and Sync on
both devices, and check that iCloud is on for Lamp in Settings under your name.

**Answers say they were written on this device.** Cloud answers is off, or there is no
connection. Turn it on in My Hub, under Account and Sync.

**Does Lamp work offline?** Yes. Everything except Cloud answers and syncing works without
a connection.
